By the time Linus Torvalds [released 4.19-rc1](<https://lwn.net/Articles/763497/>) and closed the merge window for this development cycle, 12,317 non-merge changesets had found their way into the mainline; about 4,800 of those landed after [last week's summary](<https://lwn.net/Articles/762566/>) was written. As tends to be the case late in the merge window, many of those changes were fixes for the bigger patches that went in early, but there were also a number of new features added.
